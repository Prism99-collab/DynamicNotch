# Name, logo and character

**Dynamic Notch**, its logo, and its companion character (the hacker on the neon swing) are the marks and original
creations of **Prism99-collab**.

The [MIT license](LICENSE) covers the source code. It does **not** grant any right to use the Dynamic Notch name,
logo or companion character. You may fork and change the code, but please publish your version under a different
name and with a different logo, and don't present it as the official Dynamic Notch.

## Other companies' names

Dynamic Notch works with apps and devices made by others. Their names appear in the app and in this repository only
to say what Dynamic Notch is compatible with, for example Windows, Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, Firefox, Brave,
Opera, Vivaldi, Spotify, Discord, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, WhatsApp and Steam. These names and logos belong to their
owners. Dynamic Notch isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any of them.

## Original content

All the pictures, sounds and themes that ship with Dynamic Notch are original: the theme backgrounds are drawn by
code, the notification chime is synthesised, and the companion is an original 3D character built in code. Pictures
you add yourself (the Backgrounds folder) stay on your PC and aren't part of the app.
